Censoring

Sometimes counts in administrative data are suppressed to protect people's identities. For instance, a data provider might replace any count between 1 and 4 with a blank or an asterisk, so that individuals cannot be identified. If your data has been treated this way, set the lower and upper limits of the suppressed range here, and leave the affected cells in the table blank. The calculator is designed to handle this and will still produce a valid estimate. If your data is complete, leave both values set to 0.

Method

The calculator offers two ways of producing your estimate. NPE is recommended; it produces a full picture of the uncertainty around the estimate and allows the fit of the model to be checked. But it may take a few seconds to run. NBE is a faster alternative that is useful as a quick first look.
